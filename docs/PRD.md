# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
**Dr. Noopur Patel — Breast Cancer Surgeon & Surgical Breast Oncology Website**
*Version 1.0 • Production Baseline*

---

## 1. Product Vision
To build an elite, medically responsible, and accessible digital platform for **Dr. Noopur Patel** (Breast Cancer Surgeon, Associate Consultant in Surgical Breast Oncology at Marengo CIMS Hospital, Ahmedabad).

The platform serves as:
1. An authoritative, compassionate digital identity for Dr. Noopur Patel.
2. An evidence-based breast health education and anatomy library for patients and their families.
3. A low-friction, respectful appointment consultation request channel.
4. A professional referral and peer collaboration hub for oncologists, surgeons, and physicians.

---

## 2. Core Brand Principles & Guardrails
- **Trust > Gimmicks:** Calm, clinical, editorial aesthetic. No loud popups, aggressive countdown timers, or fake urgency.
- **Strict Clinical Integrity:** No unsupported medical claims (e.g., "Best surgeon", "100% cure", "Guaranteed recovery", "Pain-free"). Only verified factual credentials.
- **Privacy by Design:** Basic appointment triage collects only non-sensitive contact details. No detailed medical history or diagnostic file uploads on public forms.
- **Inclusive & Human:** Empathetic language designed for women and families experiencing vulnerability.
